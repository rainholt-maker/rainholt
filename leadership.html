3. My Leadership Philosophy & Practice

High-Performing Teams & Friction Removal

»I build professional environments where ordinary people are given the framework to deliver extraordinary results together.«

  • Investing in people: My task as a leader is to create the conditions for skilled employees to succeed.
  • My open calendar: I consciously protect my time from superfluous operational meetings to be directly available as a sparring partner.
  • I lead from the boat: Inspired by the ancient bireme, I row along as a leader – we share the work, the responsibility, and the victories equally.

My experience as Section Head at Banedanmark

In my role as Section Head for Law & Sustainability at Banedanmark, I have implemented a strategic insourcing of civil lawsuits, where my employees conduct the cases themselves as legal representatives. This has strengthened recruitment and created significant savings with a 100% success rate in the cases conducted (AdvokatWatch).
