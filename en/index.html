1. Macroeconomics, Financial Repression & Sovereign Debt

My Analysis: $40 Trillion US Debt and National Emergency Governance

In my analysis of sovereign debt markets, I view the $40 trillion US national debt as the defining constraint for global monetary policy. With annual debt servicing exceeding $1.4 trillion USD, financial repression is deployed to administratively suppress bond yields via short-term Treasury bills. This transfers structural adjustment pressure directly to the currency, driving capital reallocation toward tangible real assets.

Ahead of the 2026 midterms, I assess that presidential emergency powers (National Emergencies Act / IEEPA) provide a rational crisis management framework for the executive branch to manage cross-border capital flows and enforce strategic economic controls.
