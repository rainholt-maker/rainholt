My analysis: $40 trillion in national debt and National Emergency as crisis management

In my analyses of the US debt market, I view the exploding national debt of over $40 trillion USD as the decisive pivot point for monetary policy in the coming years. When interest expenses exceed $1.4 trillion USD annually, the state is forced into financial repression to keep bond yields down via short T-bills. This shifts the adjustment pressure directly to the dollar and drives capital toward real assets.

Leading up to the 2026 midterm elections, I assess that presidential emergency powers (National Emergencies Act / IEEPA) constitute a rational model for the executive branch to bypass parliamentary gridlock, control capital flows, and enforce strategic control.
